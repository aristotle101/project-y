# Rewrite Playbook

For smell score 3–5. Run the sequence in order.

## Rewrite Sequence

1. Find the one thing the draft is actually trying to say. Everything else supports it.
2. Delete filler, hedges, and repeated framing before touching wording.
3. Lead with the point a person would say first out loud — not a symmetrical intro.
4. Vary sentence length. Break long clauses. Let some sentences be short.
5. Swap abstract nouns for the real thing being described.
6. Read aloud mentally — if a line has no rhythm a person would use, rewrite it.
7. Final pass: confirm register held (casual stayed casual, formal stayed formal).

## Anti-Patterns to Delete

- Tricolon overuse: "fast, reliable, and scalable" on every line
- "Not only… but also" scaffolding
- Empty transitions stacked: "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally"
- Symmetrical bullets where content isn't actually parallel
- Over-hedging: "may potentially," "could possibly help"
- Grandiose closers: "In conclusion, this represents a significant step forward"
- Em-dash pairs used as a default rhythm crutch
- Adverb + weak verb pairs ("effectively manages") → use a strong verb ("runs")
- Throat-clearing openers: "It's worth noting," "In today's world," "When it comes to"

## Quick Example

**Before (score 4):**
> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, leveraging cutting-edge solutions is essential for businesses seeking to optimize their workflows and drive meaningful results.

**After:**
> Teams move faster when their tools get out of the way. That's the whole pitch.

## Language Notes

**English:** Short sentences carry weight. Use contractions in casual/business writing. Idioms are fine when natural, not decorative.

**Thai:** ภาษาพูดกับภาษาเขียนต่างกัน — เลือกให้ตรงช่องทาง. คำลงท้าย (ครับ/ค่ะ/นะ) ช่วยให้เป็นธรรมชาติ. หลีกเลี่ยงการแปลตรงตัวจากอังกฤษ. ผสมไทย-อังกฤษได้สำหรับคำที่คนใช้จริง (meeting, deadline).

**Multilingual:** Keep code-switching only where a native speaker would. Localize punctuation and list style. Translate meaning and tone, not word order.
